## Privacy Policy and Support (English)

BESORA keeps your name, zodiac sign, age range, topics, reminder time, streak and chat only on your device. There is no account, no ads, no tracking and no data collection. A birth date entered to find the sign is not stored. Daily reminders are scheduled locally and can be turned off at any time. Sharing passes the image or text only to the app you choose. The Start over button, or removing the app, deletes everything. The content is for inspiration only and is not a forecast or medical, mental health, financial or legal advice.

If you leave your sign, age range and email on the BESORA website to hear when the app launches, they are sent to us through Netlify Forms and used only for that one email; ask for deletion at besora.app@gmail.com.
